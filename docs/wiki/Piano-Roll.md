# Piano Roll ♪

Edit the notes of a MIDI clip. Open it with a double-click on a MIDI clip (or Shift+Tab / F12 in Ableton layout, Ctrl+E in Cubase layout). Double-click an empty MIDI track to make a clip first.

| Action | How |
|---|---|
| **Draw a note** | Draw tool: click (you hear it); drag the right edge (keys below: the top edge) for its length |
| **Move** | drag a note (keys left: sideways = time, up/down = pitch; keys below: up/down = time, sideways = pitch); arrow left/right one grid step, arrow up/down one semitone |
| **Select several** | drag a frame around notes on empty space (Draw tool too: a click places a note, a drag selects), Shift/Ctrl+click, or click into the piano roll and press **Ctrl+A** |
| **Delete** | Del, or the erase tool |
| **Quantize** | Ctrl+U: the selected notes (or all) to the grid; settings (Ctrl+Shift+U): strength, swing, note ends |
| **Hear a pitch** | click a key of the keyboard (on the left, or below) |
| **Record** | **● Rec** or Ctrl+R: the song plays, and what you play on the keyboard on the left or on a MIDI keyboard becomes notes |
| **Grid** | from 1 bar to 1/64, triplets, free (Alt while dragging) |
| **Velocity** | the lane under the notes: drag a note's bar up or down (selected notes move together); or type it into *Velocity* in the toolbar while notes are selected |
| **Length** | drag the right edge of a note, or pick *Length* (1/32 to 1/1) in the toolbar for the selected notes |
| **View** | **Keys left** (as before: keys on the left, time to the right) or **🎹 Keys below**: the keyboard lies across the bottom like a real piano and the notes come down onto it, time runs upwards (the end of a note is its top edge; drag it for the length). Notes playing light up on the keys. The velocity lane is in the *Keys left* view; in both views the *Velocity* field in the toolbar sets it. The choice is remembered |
| **MIDI files** | *Export .mid* saves the clip as a MIDI file for other programs; *Import .mid…* opens a MIDI file (each of its tracks becomes a MIDI track) |

Tools: Ableton **B** toggles draw mode; Cubase **1** select, **5** erase, **8** draw.

The built-in sound is a simple synthesizer; a master limiter keeps many notes at once from distorting. Notes can come from audio: **→ MIDI** in the [[Stem Splitter]].
