# Top Bar

The bar across the top of the window, visible in every menu.

| Element | What it does |
|---|---|
| **File ▾** | **New project**, **Open…**, **Recent** (last projects), **Save** / **Save as…** (`.ams` project file), **Export audio (WAV / MP3)…** (mixes the whole song as you hear it, WAV 16/24 bit or MP3, optional normalising to −1 dBFS), **Library** |
| **↶ / ↷** | Undo / redo every change to the project (tracks, clips, notes, tempo). Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y (Cubase: Ctrl+Shift+Z) |
| **■ Stop** | Stops; pressed again, back to the start |
| **▶ Play / ❚❚ Pause** | Plays from the playhead (Space) |
| **Position** | where the playhead is: bar . beat . sixteenth (1.1.1 = the start); click: minutes:seconds |
| **● Record** (Ctrl+R) | MIDI on a MIDI track or in the Piano Roll, audio from the microphone on an audio track (count-in and punch in/out: see [[Studio]]) |
| **BPM** | The master tempo of the project: type it and press Enter (decimals work), or arrow keys / mouse wheel (Shift: 0.1). Synced audio and MIDI follow it |
| **AUTO SYNC** | Keeps all audio in time with the master BPM: switching it on syncs every audio clip now, and every new one automatically. With **several clips selected**, a click syncs them **together**: all start on the same bar. See [[Beat Matching and Sync]] |
| **Metronome** | Clicks on every beat, higher on beat 1 of a bar |
| **Time signature** | e.g. 4/4, 3/4, 6/8 (changes later in the song: right-click the ruler in the [[Studio]]) |
| **Master level** | Volume of everything, with a level meter |

**Bottom bar (status bar):** state of the AI backend, the running job with progress (click it to go back to that view), **Feedback** (see [[Feedback and Troubleshooting]]) and **Winamp (Ctrl+M)**, a simple player whose music keeps playing while you work.

**Left menu:** **«** / **»** at its top collapses it to icons or expands it with names; the choice is remembered.
