DESCRIPTION
AI Music Studio is a free, open-source AI music studio and DAW for Windows: make songs, seamless loops and stems with AI on your own PC, then arrange, mix and export them in a full studio. No account, no subscription, no upload to the cloud.

In this video you see every part of the app. Describe a style and get a full song, with vocals or as an instrumental, made by open AI models like ACE-Step and MusicGen that run locally on your computer. Need a groove for your track? "Create loop" makes loops of exactly 1 to 16 bars that start on beat 1 and repeat without a gap, named with their tempo and length, so you can line them up endlessly in the Studio.

The Studio is a real DAW with the shortcuts of Ableton Live 12 or Cubase: arrangement with markers and loop range, tempo and time-signature track, automation, warp markers, return tracks with reverb and delay, group tracks, recording with takes and comping, a mixer with level meters and a Piano Roll for MIDI.

The Stem Splitter separates any song into vocals, drums, bass, piano, guitar and more. Listen to the stems in the stem mixer, solo or mute them, export a remix such as a karaoke version without vocals, or turn a melody into MIDI notes for the Piano Roll. Every song, loop and stem is kept in the Library with tempo and tags and can be exported for any DAW as WAV, AIFF, FLAC or MP3. Mastering matches your mix to a reference track.

The built-in AI assistant helps with chords, lyrics and ideas, with Claude, ChatGPT, Grok or completely free and offline with Ollama. A hardware check shows what your PC can run: it works on the CPU alone and faster with NVIDIA, AMD or Intel graphics. New versions arrive by themselves with one click.

AI Music Studio is made by a musician for musicians and is free and open source (GPL-3.0). If you like it, give it a star on GitHub, share it, or tell me in the comments what you would like to see next.

WHAT IT DOES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
🎵 Create music: describe a style, add lyrics, pick a model and get a song with vocals, made on your own PC
🔁 Create loops: exactly 1 to 16 bars, starting on beat 1 and repeating seamlessly ("Loop 124 BPM 4 bars")
🎛️ Studio: a real DAW with loop and markers, automation, tempo track, takes, warp markers, return tracks and groups
📚 Library: every song, loop and stem with its tempo and tags, search by name, tag or BPM
💾 Export for any DAW: WAV 16/24/32-bit, AIFF, FLAC or MP3, every stem as its own file
✂️ Stem Splitter: vocals, drums, bass, piano, guitar and more, separated with Demucs, with a progress bar
🎤 Stem mixer: solo, mute and volume per stem, export a remix (karaoke without vocals, a version without drums)
🎼 Audio → MIDI: the melody of a stem as notes in the Piano Roll
🔊 Mastering: match your mix to a reference track
💬 AI Chat: a music assistant for chords, lyrics and ideas, with Claude, ChatGPT, Grok or completely free and local with Ollama
🧠 AI Engines: one-click install of ACE-Step 1.5, MusicGen, DiffRhythm, Demucs and more
🖥️ Hardware check: see what your PC can run, on CPU or NVIDIA, AMD and Intel graphics

Coming soon: VST3 plugins, Vocal Editor, AI Instruments, Karaoke Export with lyrics, Video Composer
