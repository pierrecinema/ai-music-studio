DESCRIPTION
AI Music Studio is a free, open-source AI music studio and DAW for Windows: make songs, seamless loops and stems with AI on your own PC, then arrange, mix and export them. No account, no subscription, no cloud.

Describe a style and get a full song, with vocals or instrumental, made by open AI models (ACE-Step, MusicGen) running locally. "Create loop" makes loops of exactly 1 to 16 bars that repeat without a gap.

The Studio is a real DAW with Ableton Live 12 or Cubase shortcuts: markers, tempo track, automation, warp markers, return tracks, groups, takes and a Piano Roll.

The Stem Splitter separates vocals, drums, bass and more. Solo or mute stems, export a karaoke remix, or turn a melody into MIDI. Everything is kept in the Library and exported as WAV, AIFF, FLAC or MP3.

Free and open source (GPL-3.0). A star on GitHub or a comment with your wishes helps a lot!
